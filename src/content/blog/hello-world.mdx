# Hello World

Welcome to my blog! This site has been migrated from Next.js to [Astro](https://astro.build).

## Why Astro?

Astro is a great fit for content-heavy static sites. It ships **zero JavaScript** by default,
supports Markdown and MDX out of the box, and integrates nicely with Cloudflare Pages.
